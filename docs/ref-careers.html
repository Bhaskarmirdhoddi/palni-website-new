Five Steps. No Surprises.

Here is exactly what happens after you apply.

  1. 1~5 min

    Apply

    Pick a role and send your CV. No cover letter essays required.

  2. 2~30 min

    Talk with TA

    A friendly call with our talent team about you, the role and what you want next.

  3. 3~60 min

    Technical round

    A practical conversation or exercise based on real work, not trivia.

  4. 4~45 min

    Meet your manager

    Talk goals, team and ways of working with the person you would report to.

  5. 5[Timeline]

    Offer and welcome

    A clear decision, an offer, and an onboarding plan for your first weeks.
