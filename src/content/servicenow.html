ServiceNow Transformation for Better Operations

We help enterprises improve service delivery, structure workflows and reduce avoidable manual effort. Our ServiceNow work connects platform capabilities with the processes employees, service teams and business functions rely on every day.

Start with a DiscoveryView Use Cases

Official ServiceNow Partner

Palni works with ServiceNow to help organisations improve service management and automate enterprise workflows.

Where ServiceNow Can Make a Difference

We focus on the workflows and service experiences that shape response times, operational effort and the quality of support.

ITSM & Service Operations

Structure incident, request and problem management so teams can see work clearly and follow consistent service processes.

Now Assist & AI

Use AI supported service workflows to help teams find information, prioritise work and respond with better context.

Workflow Automation

Replace avoidable manual steps with structured workflows that are easier to track, manage and improve.

Outcomes That Make ServiceNow More Effective Across the Business

A well structured ServiceNow environment makes service delivery more consistent and gives teams a clearer view of work, ownership and progress.

Faster Resolution Times

Improve the intake and routing of service work to help reduce delays and repeated effort.

Operational Efficiency

Reduce unnecessary manual steps and make workflow execution more consistent.

Scalable Service Delivery

Build processes that can support changing service volumes, teams and business requirements.

AI Enabled Operations

Apply Now Assist and other approved AI capabilities where they support service teams and workflows.

VoiceCueAI: Make Service Requests Easier to Raise

VoiceCueAI lets users report incidents and raise service requests through a voice conversation. It captures the request and connects it to structured ServiceNow workflows. This reduces friction at intake and gives service teams clearer information to act on.
